# Renewable Energies: Key to Energy Transition

Faced with the depletion of fossil fuels and the climate emergency, renewable energies are emerging as the essential solution for a sustainable future.

## Main Sources

### Solar Energy
Photovoltaic and thermal solar energy uses the sun's radiation. It is an inexhaustible and clean resource. The costs of solar panels have dropped, making this technology accessible to individuals and industries.

### Wind Energy
Onshore and offshore wind turbines transform wind power into electricity. It is one of the most mature and productive sectors.

### Hydropower
The world's leading source of renewable electricity, it uses the power of water. Although very efficient, the construction of large dams can have local ecological impacts.

### Biomass and Geothermal
Biomass (organic matter) and geothermal (earth heat) offer complementary solutions for heating and electricity production, often stable and non-intermittent.

## Challenges and Opportunities

Intermittency (the sun doesn't shine at night, the wind doesn't always blow) remains a major challenge, requiring the development of storage solutions (batteries, green hydrogen). However, the transition creates millions of green jobs and reduces countries' energy dependence.

## Conclusion

Investing in renewables is not just an ecological choice, it is an economic and strategic necessity to guarantee a livable world for future generations.
