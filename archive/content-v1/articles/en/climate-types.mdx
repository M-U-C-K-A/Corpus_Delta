# Climate Types

## Classification of Climates

Climates are classified based on temperature and precipitation. Major types include tropical, arid, temperate, and polar climates.

## Adaptation Strategies

Understanding climate types helps in developing adaptation strategies. For instance, arid regions focus on water conservation, while coastal areas prepare for sea-level rise.
