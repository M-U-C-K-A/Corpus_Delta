# Transport Carbon Footprint: Moving Without Warming

The transport sector represents about **16% of global GHG emissions** and 24% of energy-related emissions. It's also one of the sectors where emissions have grown most since 1990.

## Distribution by Mode

**Road transport**: 74% of sector emissions, dominated by passenger cars and trucks.

**Aviation**: 12% of transport emissions, but growing rapidly and with climate impact beyond CO₂ alone (contrails, ozone).

**Shipping**: 10% of emissions, mainly international freight.

**Rail**: Only 1%, the most efficient mode for long distances.

## Comparing Modes

In grams of CO₂ per passenger-kilometer, the orders of magnitude are: long-haul flight (150-200 g), car alone (150 g), carpooling (50-75 g), bus (30-50 g), train (5-30 g), e-bike (5 g), bicycle (0 g).

## Solutions for Road Transport

**Electric vehicles**: Zero emissions at use, and even with current electricity, less emitting over their life cycle.

**Shared mobility**: Carpooling, car sharing, public transit.

**Active mobility**: Cycling, walking, scooters for short distances.

**Urban planning**: Reduce distances through compact, mixed-use cities.

## The Aviation Challenge

No mature technical solution in the short term. Sustainable aviation fuels (SAF) limited in volume. Only immediate lever: reduce the number of flights, especially short-haul routes replaceable by train.

## Maritime Transport

Heavy fuel oil is the worst fuel in terms of emissions. Alternatives: LNG (moderate gain), green methanol, ammonia, modern sails, and speed optimization.
