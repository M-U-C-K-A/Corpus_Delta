# Circular Economy: Zero Waste, Zero Emissions

The circular economy breaks from the linear "extract-make-dispose" model to keep resources in circulation as long as possible. It's a powerful but underestimated lever in the climate fight.

## Link to Climate

Material extraction and processing represent about **70% of global GHG emissions**. Circular economy = fewer primary materials = fewer emissions.

Aluminum recycling uses 95% less energy than primary production. Recycled steel: 75% less. Glass: 30% less.

## Fundamental Principles

**Eliminate waste by design**: Products designed to be repaired, reused, recycled.

**Circulate products and materials**: Rental, sharing, secondhand, refurbishment, recycling.

**Regenerate natural systems**: Regenerative agriculture, composting, soil restoration.

## Strategies (the "Rs")

**Refuse** what's not necessary. **Reduce** consumption. **Reuse** products. **Repair** rather than discard. **Repurpose** for another use. **Recycle** as a last resort.

## Concrete Examples

**Refurbished phones**: Save 50-80% of emissions compared to new.

**Circular fashion**: Clothing rental, resale, upcycling.

**Construction**: Reuse of building materials.

## Barriers and Levers

The linear economy often remains cheaper because environmental externalities aren't priced. Taxing virgin resources and facilitating recycling can reverse this.
