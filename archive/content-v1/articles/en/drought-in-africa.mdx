# Drought in Africa

## Challenges of Drought

Africa faces severe drought challenges, exacerbated by climate change. Water scarcity impacts agriculture, livestock, and daily life for millions of people.

## Building Resilience

Communities are adopting resilient agricultural practices and water conservation techniques. International support and local initiatives are crucial for adapting to these changing climate conditions.
