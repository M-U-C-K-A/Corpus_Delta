# Loss of Biodiversity

## Impact on Ecosystems

Biodiversity loss affects ecosystems and human well-being. Species extinction rates are now hundreds of times higher than natural rates.

## Data on Biodiversity Loss

Data shows drastic declines in wildlife populations, with significant impacts on ecosystem services such as pollination and water purification.
