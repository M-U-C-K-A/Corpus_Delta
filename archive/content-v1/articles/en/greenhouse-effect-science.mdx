# The Greenhouse Effect: Physical and Chemical Mechanisms

The greenhouse effect is a natural process essential for life on Earth, maintaining a habitable average temperature of 15°C. Without it, the temperature would plummet to around -18°C. However, the anthropogenic intensification of this phenomenon is the primary cause of current global warming.

## Earth's Earth's Radiative Balance

The engine of Earth's climate is the balance between energy received from the Sun and energy returned to space.

![Greenhouse Effect Diagram](/articles/greenhouse-effect-diagram.png)

1.  **Incoming Solar Radiation**: The Sun emits radiation primarily in the visible and near-infrared spectrum. About 340 W/m² reaches the top of the atmosphere.
2.  **Absorption and Reflection**: About 30% of this energy is reflected by clouds, aerosols, and the Earth's surface (albedo). The remaining 70% is absorbed by the surface and the atmosphere, warming the planet.
3.  **Outgoing Infrared Radiation**: The warmed Earth re-emits energy in the form of thermal infrared radiation (longer wavelengths).

## The Role of Greenhouse Gases (GHGs)

Greenhouse gases (H₂O, CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, O₃) are transparent to visible solar radiation but opaque to infrared radiation emitted by the Earth.

### Molecular Absorption
At the quantum scale, GHG molecules absorb infrared photons, which excites their vibration/rotation modes. Upon returning to their ground state, they re-emit this energy in all directions, including towards the Earth's surface. This "radiative forcing" traps heat in the lower atmosphere (troposphere).

### Main Anthropogenic GHGs
*   **Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)**: The main driver of warming (66% of radiative forcing). Its concentration has risen from 280 ppm (pre-industrial) to over 420 ppm today.
*   **Methane (CH₄)**: Less abundant but 28 times more potent than CO₂ over 100 years. Emitted by agriculture, landfills, and fossil fuel exploitation.
*   **Nitrous Oxide (N₂O)**: 265 times more potent than CO₂, primarily emitted by nitrogen fertilizers.

## Saturation of the Greenhouse Effect?

A common misconception is that the greenhouse effect is "saturated" because CO₂ already absorbs all radiation in its spectral bands. This is false. Adding CO₂ optically thickens the atmosphere, pushing the effective emission altitude to higher, colder layers, which reduces the efficiency of cooling to space and warms the surface.
