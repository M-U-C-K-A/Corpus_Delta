# World Hunger

## Causes of Hunger

Climate change exacerbates food insecurity by disrupting agriculture. Extreme weather events destroy crops and livestock, leading to food shortages.

## Solutions to Hunger

Sustainable farming practices and resilient crop varieties are essential. International aid and policy changes can also play a crucial role in addressing hunger.
