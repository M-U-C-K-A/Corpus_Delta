# The Arctic in Crisis: The Sentinel of Warming

The Arctic is warming 2 to 4 times faster than the rest of the planet. This "Arctic amplification" makes the region the best indicator—and first accelerator—of global climate change.

## Alarming Observations

**Sea ice**: Summer minimum extent has decreased by more than 40% since 1979. Average thickness has dropped by more than 2 meters. An ice-free Arctic summer is likely before 2050.

**Permafrost**: Millions of km² of soils frozen for millennia are beginning to thaw, destabilizing infrastructure and releasing carbon and methane.

**Greenland ice sheet**: Loss of 270 Gt of ice per year, contributing significantly to sea level rise.

**Ecosystems**: Arctic species (polar bears, walruses, seals) are seeing their habitat disappear.

## Why the Arctic Warms Faster

The ice-albedo feedback amplifies initial warming. Sea ice loss exposes dark ocean that absorbs more heat. Changes in atmospheric and oceanic circulation also contribute.

## Human Impacts

Indigenous peoples see their traditional ways of life threatened. Hunting, fishing, and travel on ice become dangerous or impossible. Coastal villages are eroding and must be relocated.

## Global Consequences

Contribution to sea level rise, carbon release from permafrost, jet stream disruption affecting mid-latitude weather, and opening of shipping routes increasing traffic and risks.

## Solutions

Drastic reduction of global emissions (the only way to limit Arctic warming), support for Arctic communities for adaptation, and protection of Arctic marine areas.
