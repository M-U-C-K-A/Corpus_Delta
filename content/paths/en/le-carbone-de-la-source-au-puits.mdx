About half the CO₂ emitted each year disappears from the atmosphere. Knowing where it goes,
and on what conditions it will keep going there, is one of the most decisive questions in
the climate file — and one of the least well measured.

This path follows carbon in physical order: the measurement first, the accounting next,
then each of the two great reservoirs. It ends on what oceanic uptake costs the ocean
itself, because a service rendered is not free.
