Uncertainty is the argument most used to defer action, and the most misunderstood. A wide
range does not say that nothing is known: it says the bounds of the possible are known,
and those bounds are often enough to decide on.

This path starts from the vocabulary of uncertainty, passes through what it is worth in
scenarios and in pledges, then reaches the case where it stops being neutral. It assumes
at least one discovery path has been read first.
