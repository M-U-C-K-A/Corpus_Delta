Attribution studies have become the standard answer to the question asked after every
heatwave. They are also among the most poorly reported, because their result is a
probability and a probability is easily read as a certainty.

This path follows the order in which the notions hold together: first what a rare event
is, then how a signal is separated from noise, finally what the method establishes. It
ends with the language the results are written in, which is the last opportunity to get
them wrong.
