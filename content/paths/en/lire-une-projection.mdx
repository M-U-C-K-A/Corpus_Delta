A climate projection is not a long-range weather forecast. This path sets out to make the
fanned-out charts you meet everywhere legible, and above all to make clear which question
each of them can — and cannot — settle.

<Callout variant="info" title="Set aside an hour">
This path assumes you have already met the notion of the greenhouse effect. If not, start
with "Understanding warming in an hour".
</Callout>
