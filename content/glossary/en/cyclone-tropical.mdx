The three names denote the same phenomenon; only the ocean basin differs. Formation
requires surface water warm enough over a sufficient depth, together with weak wind shear
aloft.

<Callout variant="info" title="More intense does not mean more numerous">
The best-established signal is not about frequency — which may even fall slightly — but
about the **proportion of cyclones reaching the highest categories**, the rainfall they
deliver, and the height of the surges, made worse by rising
<Term id="niveau-marin">sea level</Term>.
</Callout>

Damage rarely comes from wind alone: coastal surge and intense rainfall usually account for
most of it.
