Adaptation deals with consequences; <Term id="attenuation">mitigation</Term> deals with
causes. Both are needed and neither substitutes for the other: some warming is already
locked in whatever happens, and no amount of adaptation can keep pace with unlimited warming.

It spans very different registers: protective works, revised building codes, warning
systems, crop variety choices in agriculture, reorganised care during heat episodes.

<Callout variant="caution" title="Maladaptation">
An adaptation measure can worsen vulnerability over time: a dyke that encourages building
on a floodplain, widespread air conditioning that heats the street and raises electricity
demand. This is a documented risk, not a theoretical objection.
</Callout>
