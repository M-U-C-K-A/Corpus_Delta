The definition turns on one word: **anthropogenic**. The natural sinks that already absorb
part of our emissions do not enter the equation; only removals resulting from deliberate
action are counted.

That is what separates a credible trajectory from an accounting exercise. Relying on
existing forest to "neutralise" emissions amounts to claiming credit for a service that was
already there, and that owes nothing to the effort made.

<Callout variant="caution" title="Neutrality of an entity, neutrality of the planet">
Neutrality has a physical meaning at global scale. Applied to a company or a product, it
rests on offsetting mechanisms whose permanence and additionality are often hard to
establish. The two uses of the word do not carry the same guarantees.
</Callout>
