Vulnerability explains why the same hazard produces such unequal consequences. A heatwave of
comparable intensity does not kill in the same proportions depending on the age of the
population, the quality of the building stock, the social isolation of those affected and
the organisation of health services.

It is the factor most directly reducible by public action, and the one that makes the human
toll of disasters largely independent of their physical intensity.
