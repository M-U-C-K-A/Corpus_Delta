The framework places climate among other processes on which the stability of the Earth system
depends: biosphere integrity, the nitrogen and phosphorus cycles, land use, fresh water,
ocean acidification, stratospheric ozone, aerosols, novel entities
<Cite id="steffen-2015-planetary-boundaries-guiding-human-development" />.

Its main value is to show that these processes interact. Deforestation releases carbon and
reduces biodiversity; intensive agriculture disturbs the nitrogen cycle and transforms soils.
Treating climate in isolation means ignoring those couplings.

<Callout variant="uncertainty" title="A contested framework">
The thresholds proposed are not all established with equal confidence, and some researchers
dispute the very idea of a numerical limit for processes whose response is gradual. The
framework remains useful as a way of reading the problem, less so as an exact measure.
</Callout>
