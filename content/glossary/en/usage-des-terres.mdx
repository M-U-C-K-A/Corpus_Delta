Converting a forest to cropland releases the carbon stored in the biomass and in the soil,
and permanently removes a <Term id="puits-de-carbone">sink</Term>. The reverse — replanting,
restoring a peatland — can create one.

It is the least well-constrained line of the global
<Term id="budget-carbone">carbon budget</Term>. Fossil emissions are derived from relatively
reliable energy statistics; land-related flows rest on forest inventories, remote sensing
and assumptions about soil carbon stocks, with far wider uncertainty.
