The term does not denote a burnt area above some threshold, but a fire regime: an intensity
at which ground and air resources become useless, and the fire generates its own winds, even
its own thunderclouds.

Climate does not start these fires; almost all ignitions are human. It acts on the conditions
that make them uncontrollable: vegetation dried by a prolonged
<Term id="secheresse">drought</Term>, high temperatures, low humidity, wind. That combination
is a textbook <Term id="evenement-compose">compound event</Term>.

<Callout variant="info" title="A feedback onto carbon">
A forest that burns releases the carbon it stored and stops being a
<Term id="puits-de-carbone">sink</Term> for years. Fires are thus among the mechanisms by
which warming weakens the sinks that moderate it

</Callout>
