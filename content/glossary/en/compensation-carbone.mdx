The principle assumes that a tonne emitted here is equivalent to a tonne avoided elsewhere.
Three conditions must then hold, and each is hard to establish.

**Additionality**: the funded reduction would not have happened without the payment. A forest
that was not going to be cut offsets nothing. **Permanence**: the carbon must stay stored for
as long as it would have remained in the atmosphere, meaning centuries. A fire cancels a
planting. **No leakage**: the avoided activity must not simply move elsewhere.

<Callout variant="caution" title="What offsetting cannot do">
At global scale the arithmetic fails: not everyone can offset against everyone else.
Offsetting can fund worthwhile projects, but it does not replace cutting emissions at source,
the only lever whose effect is certain

</Callout>
