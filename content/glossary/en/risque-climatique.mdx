## Three factors, not one

The framework adopted by the IPCC breaks risk into three terms that multiply together.

The **hazard** is the physical phenomenon: a flood, a heatwave, a cyclone. **Exposure**
denotes what lies in its path — inhabitants, infrastructure, activities.
<Term id="vulnerabilite">Vulnerability</Term> describes how prone those elements are to
being harmed.

The value of this decomposition is operational: if any one of the three terms is zero, the
risk is too. A cyclone over an empty ocean is not a risk. And crucially, two of the three
factors — exposure and vulnerability — follow from human decisions, and so offer immediate
room for action, independent of the climate trajectory.
