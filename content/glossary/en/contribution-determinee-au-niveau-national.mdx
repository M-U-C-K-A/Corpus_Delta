## A pledge the state sets itself

The <Term id="accord-de-paris">Paris Agreement</Term> does not allocate targets between
countries. It asks each to declare its own, publish it, and revise it every five years in the
direction of greater ambition only. This is the so-called **ratchet** mechanism.

That choice was the price of the 2015 consensus: a treaty imposing quotas would not have been
ratified. It bears directly on how the numbers should be read. The sum of NDCs is not a climate
trajectory but an aggregate of sovereign intentions, whose legal force varies by country and whose
implementation is not guaranteed.

Hence the constant distinction in the literature between what states **promise**, what their
already-enacted policies **would deliver**, and what the remaining
<Term id="budget-carbone">carbon budget</Term> would require. The three trajectories do not
coincide, and taking the first for the others is the commonest misreading.
