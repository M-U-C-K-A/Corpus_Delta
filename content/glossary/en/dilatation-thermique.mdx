The ocean takes up the great majority of the excess energy retained by the climate system.
That heat expands the water: without a single drop being added, the volume grows and the
level rises.

This mechanism explains the inertia of the phenomenon. Heat takes centuries to reach the
deep layers, and once absorbed it does not leave quickly. Even at zero emissions, the rise
from expansion would therefore continue for a long time — what is known as the warming
already committed.
