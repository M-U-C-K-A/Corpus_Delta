## Immense flows, a minuscule imbalance

Every year, vegetation and the ocean exchange with the atmosphere quantities of carbon far
larger than human emissions. Those flows roughly balanced before the industrial era: what
was absorbed was returned.

Fossil emissions are modest against those exchanges, but nothing offsets them. They add
carbon long locked underground to a system that ran as a closed loop. It is that imbalance,
not the size of the flows, that drives concentration up.
