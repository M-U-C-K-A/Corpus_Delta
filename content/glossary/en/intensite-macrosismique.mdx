One earthquake produces **as many intensity values as there are places to observe it**:
high directly above the source, decreasing with distance, and modulated by the nature of
the ground and the quality of construction.

Intensity is inferred from testimony and field survey, not from an instrument. That is what
makes it the only way to characterise earthquakes predating instrumental seismology, from
archives.

A moderate <Term id="magnitude-sismique">magnitude</Term> at shallow depth beneath an
inhabited area can produce higher intensities than a far larger magnitude occurring
offshore.
