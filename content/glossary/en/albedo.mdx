## A feedback that sustains itself

Fresh snow and sea ice are among the most reflective natural surfaces; ice-free ocean is
among the most absorbing. That contrast drives a well-documented feedback: warming melts
ice, the dark surface it uncovers absorbs more solar energy, which sharpens local warming
and accelerates the melt.

It is one of the mechanisms behind the Arctic warming markedly faster than the global
average. The same logic, reversed, applies to artificial light surfaces: painting roofs
white raises their albedo and reduces heat absorption at the scale of a building.
