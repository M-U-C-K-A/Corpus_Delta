## What RCPs named, and what SSPs added

RCPs are the scenarios of the IPCC's fifth report, published in 2013. Each is named for
the <Term id="forcage-radiatif">radiative forcing</Term> it reaches in 2100: RCP2.6 for 2.6 W/m²,
RCP8.5 for 8.5. The name therefore designates a **physical endpoint**, not a socio-economic story.

That was precisely what they lacked. The same forcing can come from a populous world with low
per-head emissions, or the reverse; RCPs did not say which, which made it hard to assess societal
vulnerability or policy cost. The <Term id="scenario-ssp">SSPs</Term> of the sixth report added that
dimension: an SSP combines a demographic, economic and technological trajectory with a forcing
level, hence two-number labels such as SSP2-4.5.

RCPs remain very common in the literature published between 2013 and 2021, and the two families
overlap only approximately. Reading a study from that period means knowing which it uses: treating
an RCP8.5 and an SSP5-8.5 as the same object introduces a silent error.
