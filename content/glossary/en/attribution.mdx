## A question of probability, not of single causes

Attribution does not try to establish that a particular event "was caused by" climate
change — a formulation with no physical meaning, since every weather phenomenon results
from a combination of factors.

The method compares two worlds: the observed world, and a simulated world without human
influence on the climate. The probability of the event occurring is estimated in each, and
the ratio between the two quantifies the human influence. A typical conclusion takes the
form "this episode was *n* times more likely" or "*x* degrees more intense" than it would
have been without warming.

The approach works well for <Term id="canicule">heatwaves</Term>, whose physical link to
warming is direct, and much less well for localised, rare phenomena such as tornadoes.
