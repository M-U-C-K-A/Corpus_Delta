The word covers four phenomena that are best not confused, failing which two people can
argue while each relies on a different indicator.

**Meteorological** drought is a rainfall deficit, over a few weeks. **Agricultural** drought
concerns soil moisture in the root zone, and depends as much on
<Term id="evapotranspiration">evapotranspiration</Term> as on rainfall. **Hydrological**
drought affects river flows and groundwater, with several months' lag and slow recovery.
**Socio-economic** drought arrives when the resource no longer meets demand — so it also
depends on that demand.

A single wet winter can end the first while resolving nothing of the third.
