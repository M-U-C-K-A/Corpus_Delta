## The horizon changes everything

GWP makes it possible to bring different gases to a common unit. But it depends entirely on
the period over which the effect is integrated, and that period is a **choice**, not a
physical property.

Over a hundred years — the most common convention — methane appears markedly more powerful
than CO₂. Over twenty years the gap is wider still, because methane has not had time to
dissipate. Over five hundred years it nearly vanishes.

The choice of horizon is therefore not neutral: it decides the relative weight of cattle
farming against a coal plant in the same inventory. It is a defensible accounting
convention, provided it is stated.
