An earthquake originates at depth, at the **focus** — or hypocentre. The epicentre is only
its projection at the surface: two earthquakes with the same epicentre and the same
<Term id="magnitude-sismique">magnitude</Term> can produce very different effects depending
on how deep the focus lies.

A shallow focus concentrates the energy over a small area and produces high
<Term id="intensite-macrosismique">intensities</Term>. A deep focus spreads the energy over
a wider area, felt far away but less violent directly above.

This is why seismological bulletins always give both: the position of the epicentre and the
depth of the focus. The second is as decisive as the first in anticipating damage.
