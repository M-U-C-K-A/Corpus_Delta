A prolonged drought followed by a <Term id="canicule">heatwave</Term>, heavy rain on
already saturated ground, a storm surge coinciding with a river flood: in each case, the
impact is not the sum of the individual impacts.

The methodological stake is significant. Conventional risk analyses assess each hazard
separately and implicitly assume they are independent. When hazards are correlated — which
is common, since they often share the same atmospheric causes — that approach
**systematically underestimates** the probability of the most damaging situations.
