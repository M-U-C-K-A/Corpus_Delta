## The mechanism without which Earth would be frozen

Sunlight, mostly in the visible range, passes through the atmosphere and heats the surface.
The surface re-emits that energy as infrared radiation, which certain gases — water vapour,
CO₂, methane — absorb and re-emit in all directions, including downwards.

The share returned to the surface keeps Earth at a temperature compatible with liquid
water. The natural greenhouse effect is therefore not the problem: it is its
**strengthening** by gases of human origin that shifts the balance.

<Callout variant="caution" title="The greenhouse metaphor misleads">
A garden greenhouse works mainly by blocking convection — warm air cannot escape. The
atmosphere acts on radiation instead. The analogy fixed the name; it does not explain the
mechanism.
</Callout>
