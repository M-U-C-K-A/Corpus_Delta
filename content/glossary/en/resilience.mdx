Resilience is neither resistance — taking the blow without changing — nor a simple return
to the previous state. It includes reorganisation: a resilient system can emerge from a
disturbance transformed while still delivering what matters.

The word is heavily used, sometimes to the point of losing its meaning. It stays useful on
one condition: specify the resilience **of what**, **to what**, and **over what timescale**.
Without those three, it says nothing verifiable.

Beyond a certain threshold, the capacity to reorganise stops working: that is what the
notion of a <Term id="point-de-bascule">tipping point</Term> denotes.
