Coral lives in symbiosis with microscopic algae that supply most of its energy and give it
its colour. Beyond a certain thermal threshold the partnership breaks down: the algae are
expelled, and the white calcareous skeleton shows through.

A bleached coral is not a dead coral. If the temperature falls again quickly enough, the
symbiosis can re-establish itself. What kills is the **duration** of the stress, and the
repetition of episodes without time to recover — which is why
<Term id="canicule">marine heatwaves</Term> matter more than the underlying trend alone.
