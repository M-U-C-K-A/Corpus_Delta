Mitigation acts on the cause: fewer emissions, or more absorption. It is routinely confused
with <Term id="adaptation">adaptation</Term>, which acts on the consequences — the two are
complementary, not interchangeable.

The levers fall into three families: reducing demand for a service, improving the
efficiency with which it is met, and substituting low-carbon sources for fossil ones.
Scenarios compatible with the Paris Agreement draw on all three; none rests on a single
technology.
