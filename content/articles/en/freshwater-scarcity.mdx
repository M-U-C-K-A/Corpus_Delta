# Freshwater Scarcity: Global Water Stress

Freshwater represents less than 3% of water on Earth, and most is trapped in glaciers. Climate change profoundly alters the water cycle, worsening shortages in many regions.

## Current State

More than **2 billion people** live in water-stressed countries. This number could reach 5 billion by 2050. Agriculture accounts for 70% of global freshwater consumption.

## Climate Change Impacts

### Precipitation Changes
Dry regions are generally getting drier, while wet regions get wetter. Variability is increasing: more droughts and more floods.

### Glacier Melting
Mountain "water towers" supply billions of people (Himalayas, Andes, Alps). Their gradual disappearance threatens summer supply when rains are lacking.

### Increased Evaporation
Higher temperatures increase evaporation from reservoirs, soils, and crops, reducing effective water availability.

## Critical Regions

The Mediterranean basin, Middle East, Central Asia, northern China, southwestern United States, and central Chile are among the most at-risk regions.

## Solutions

**Efficiency**: Drip irrigation, wastewater recycling, less water-intensive industries.

**Storage**: Reservoirs, groundwater recharge, rainwater harvesting.

**Desalination**: Technical solution but energy-intensive.

**Governance**: Transboundary cooperation on shared river basins.

**Pricing**: Water prices reflecting true value to encourage conservation.
