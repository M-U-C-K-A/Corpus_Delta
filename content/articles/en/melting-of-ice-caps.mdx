# Melting of Ice Caps

## Data on Ice Melting

The melting of ice caps has accelerated over the past decades. Current statistics show a significant reduction in ice volume, contributing to rising sea levels and disrupting marine ecosystems.

## Consequences of Ice Melting

Environmental impacts include habitat loss for polar species and increased coastal erosion. Populations living in low-lying areas face heightened risks of flooding and displacement.
