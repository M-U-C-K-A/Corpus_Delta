# Paleoclimatology: The Archives of Earth's Climate

Paleoclimatology studies past climates to understand how the climate system works. This science provides valuable "analogs" to anticipate future changes.

## Climate Archives

![Paleoclimate Timeline](/articles/paleoclimate.png)

### Ice Cores
Air bubbles trapped in Greenland and Antarctic ice preserve past atmosphere:
- **EPICA Dome C**: 800,000 years of history
- **Measurements**: CO₂, CH₄, temperature (isotopes δ¹⁸O)
- **Resolution**: Annual (recent millennia), decadal (older)

### Ocean Sediments
Foraminifera (micro-organisms) in sediments record:
- Ocean temperature
- Ice volume
- Ocean circulation

### Other Archives
- **Tree rings**: Precipitation, temperature (recent millennia)
- **Speleothems**: Caves, monsoons
- **Corals**: Tropical SSTs

## Major Climate Eras

### Quaternary (2.6 Ma - present)
- Alternation of **glaciations** (100,000 years) and **interglacials** (10-20,000 years)
- Last Glacial Maximum (LGM) 21,000 years ago: -5°C, sea level -120 m
- Holocene (11,700 years - present): Stable climate, human civilizations

### Eocene (56-34 Ma)
- "Hothouse" climate: CO₂ > 1000 ppm, no polar ice caps
- Arctic: Forests, crocodiles

### PETM (56 Ma)
- Massive carbon release (~5000 GtC over several millennia)
- 5-8°C warming
- Ocean acidification, marine extinctions

## Lessons for Today

| Event | Duration | CO₂ | ΔT | Lesson |
|-------|----------|-----|----|---------|
| PETM | ~20,000 years | +1000 ppm | +5-8°C | Severe ocean impacts |
| LGM→Holocene | ~10,000 years | +100 ppm | +5°C | ~1°C/2000 years natural |
| Anthropocene | ~150 years | +140 ppm | +1.2°C | 10-100x faster |

## Key Message

**The current rate of change is unprecedented** in the geological record. Ecosystems have never had to adapt this rapidly.
