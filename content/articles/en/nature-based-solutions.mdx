# Nature-Based Solutions: When Nature Works for Us

Nature-based solutions (NbS) use natural or restored ecosystems to address climate challenges. They offer significant co-benefits for biodiversity, water, and local communities.

## Definition

NbS are actions that protect, sustainably manage, or restore natural ecosystems to address societal challenges—including climate change—effectively and adaptively, while ensuring human well-being and biodiversity.

## Types of NbS for Climate

**Protection of existing forests**: Preventing deforestation avoids massive emissions and preserves carbon sinks.

**Reforestation and afforestation**: Planting trees sequesters CO₂. But plant diverse forests, not monocultures.

**Wetland restoration**: Peatlands, mangroves, salt marshes store carbon and protect coasts.

**Regenerative agriculture**: Improving agricultural soils.

**Seagrass restoration**: These underwater meadows store carbon and harbor marine life.

## Sequestration Potential

NbS could contribute up to **10-30% of the mitigation needed** to limit warming to 1.5-2°C. That's not negligible, but it doesn't replace reducing fossil emissions.

## Additional Benefits

Flood and erosion protection, water purification, biodiversity habitat, local livelihoods, and mental health and well-being of populations.

## Warning

NbS must not serve as a pretext to avoid emissions reductions. They complement decarbonization; they don't replace it.
