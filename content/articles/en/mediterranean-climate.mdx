# The Mediterranean: A Climate Hotspot

The Mediterranean basin is warming 20% faster than the global average. This region, cradle of ancient civilizations, is one of the most vulnerable "hotspots" to climate change.

## Regional Observations

**Temperature**: +1.5°C since the pre-industrial era, slightly above the global average.

**Precipitation**: 10-20% decline in rainfall since the 1950s, with a worsening trend.

**Sea level**: About +3 mm/year, threatening low-lying coastal cities.

## Multiple Impacts

**Droughts**: More frequent, intense, and longer. Southern Spain, southern Italy, and the Maghreb are particularly affected.

**Wildfires**: Extended fire season, increased intensity. Greece, Portugal, and Algeria have experienced devastating fires.

**Heat waves**: Over 45°C regularly reached in summer. Temperature records broken every year.

**Water stress**: Agriculture, tourism, and urban populations compete for a shrinking resource.

**Marine biodiversity**: Mediterranean warming, arrival of invasive tropical species, mortality of sensitive invertebrates.

## Vulnerable Populations

Mediterranean agriculture (olives, vines, citrus), fishing, beach tourism, and populations of southern countries less equipped for adaptation.

## Solutions

Integrated water management, adapted agriculture (resistant varieties, efficient irrigation), fire prevention, building thermal renovation, and urban planning resilient to extreme heat.
